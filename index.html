Device stays in download mode after installing?

ESP32-S3 boards that enter the bootloader from the running firmware keep a "force download" flag set, so the automatic reset after installing lands in the bootloader again. This button clears the flag and reboots MeowKit.

Fallback: hold the power button until MeowKit switches off, then switch it on again.

Installing factory or local test firmware may reset device settings. Do not disconnect the USB cable during installation.
